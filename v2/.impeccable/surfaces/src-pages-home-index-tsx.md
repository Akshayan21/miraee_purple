# V2 home page (Persuade)

Scope: the V2 home page at `/v2/`, content word for word from V1. Visitor: finance and travel leads at companies, plus the general reader. Success: sign up free, or ask for a spend review.

Audience, proof and constraints: no invented customers, claims or figures; product views carry illustrative data; one video only (the closing band, V1 content); route line and plane are removed (owner decision); one real WebGL 3D object with a CSS/static fallback; reduced-motion and small screens get a calm static page.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Quiet confidence. One idea per screen, large type, generous white space and one real 3D object, in the manner of Stripe, Mercury and Apple's AirPods Pro page. It refuses the previous V2's busy, always-moving, dark and video-heavy collage.

OWN-WORLD: The category standard played straight (standing exit), at full fidelity. Light grounds (white and a faint lilac tint) carry most of the page; deep plum (#250918) is reserved for two moments; tangerine orange only for primary actions and the single circled figures. Archivo display at 700 with tight tracking, hairline rules, soft long shadows under product views, tabular numerals. The 3D object is a plum corporate card with a clearcoat finish.

STORY: The visitor understands in one viewport that Miraee puts every trip, with its budget and GL code, in one place; scrolls through how it works, proof first, what happens when plans change, and why finance and the whole company can trust it; and signs up free or asks for a spend review.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left, a two-line display headline "Business travel. Every dollar in view." at up to 96px, the lead, two pill buttons (Sign up free, Get a spend review) and the small free-to-sign-up note. Right, a large plum 3D corporate card floating on a soft lilac glow. The floating pill nav sits on top. As you scroll, the card turns over and drifts while the copy stays; no video.

FORM: Canvas world, the standing exit: a clean SaaS landing at Stripe / Mercury / Apple craft. Position 4 of the seven grounded candidates, assigned roll was candidate 5 (Visa pages); the user took the standing exit. Seed key 932aeefb. Raises carried from declined challengers: hierarchy by scale alone, no boxes (variable-font specimen); one statement per scene (glazier partition); orange reserved for what matters (tensegrity cord).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
